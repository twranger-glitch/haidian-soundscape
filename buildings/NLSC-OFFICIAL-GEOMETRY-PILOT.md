# NLSC official geometry pilot — dev37.9.7 expansion

This local pilot contains 419 official NLSC I3S building geometries with official reported BUILD_H values.
It is additive positive shade evidence only. Dataset absence MUST NOT be interpreted as open sky or direct sun, and no completeness proof is claimed.
The first 104 features were the dev37.9.6 pilot. 315 additional real NLSC geometries were discovered around remaining dev37.9.6 building-height-unknown samples and validated by the existing ASTRA shade engine in a read-only diagnostic.
