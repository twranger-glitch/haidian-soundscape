# ASTRA dev37.9.9.8 — Generic Realm Walkability Confidence

Baseline: live-accepted dev37.9.9.7.1.

This release replaces the former binary `source-supported vs synthetic-blocked` treatment for public Realm geometry with a three-level walkability model while preserving provenance truth:

1. **verified** — existing mapped pedestrian geometry or independently source-supported pedestrian geometry;
2. **inferred-open-space** — obstacle-checked geometry inside a conservative public open-space Realm, or a bounded Realm→mapped-network portal transition; always marked as inferred and requiring on-site confirmation;
3. **unresolved / blocked** — any remaining synthetic geometry, incomplete safety/source acquisition, private/no/foot=no conflicts, review-only realms, terminal connectors, holes, interior concave excursions, obstacle/barrier crossings, or untrusted inferred claims.

Only `public-park` and `public-village-green` are inference-eligible in this release. Explicit pedestrian areas remain source-supported through the pre-existing provenance classifier; garden/recreation-ground and institutional/facility areas remain review-only. Inference never relabels geometry as OSM footway, official sidewalk, or verified surface.

## Comparator policy

`source-supported` and `inferred-open-space` candidates may enter exposure comparison when unresolved synthetic distance is zero and all existing detour/quality/source rules pass. The existing uncertainty-safe exposure comparator is unchanged: a winner is valid only when its direct-sun upper bound plus the existing tie margin is below every competitor lower bound. An inferred winner remains `requiresOnSitePathConfirmation=true` and the UI explicitly labels it as inferred.

## Generic safety gates

Open-space inference requires the same resolved Realm area id, complete acquired safety geometry, auto-eligible public Realm classification, no blocked access, full segment membership under the existing Realm boundary policy, and a fresh building/water/barrier visibility veto. Holes are always excluded. Exact-outside intervals caused by concave interior excursions are rejected; the existing 1.8 m Realm membership tolerance may only absorb endpoint/boundary-source imprecision and does not become source proof. Terminal connectors remain non-inferable. Portal transitions retain the pre-existing bounded attach limit (7 m open-boundary / configured explicit portal limit) and are rechecked against obstacles/barriers.
